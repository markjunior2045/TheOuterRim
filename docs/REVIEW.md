# Interpretations awaiting the owner's review

Readings made while encoding cards or rules where the text left room for doubt. The engine follows the
reading below until the owner confirms or corrects it. When an item is settled, move the decision to
`RULES.md` (or the card data) and delete it here.

Cards are named `Name-num` (image number), as in `data/transcription/`.

| # | Where | Reading in use | Since |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | R-8.4, R-8.2 (health or hull goes down) | When a character's health or a ship's hull goes down below the damage on it (Armored Vest-3 or Chewbacca's +health lost with the card), the tokens above the new value are removed ("cannot hold more damage", R-8.4). If the damage is then **equal** to the new value, the player becomes defeated (R-8.2 read literally: "when a character has damage equal to its health"). Applied once the operation in progress has finished. Found by the V0 games: Han Solo-4 with Chewbacca took 5 damage, lost Chewbacca to a bounty and was left with 5 damage on health 4. | 2026-11-05 (T3.1) |
| 2 | Chromium Hull (luxury-01), "When you gain fame, gain 1 additional fame" | +1 fame **per gain**, whatever its size (a reward of 2 fame gives 3), and only on gains: extra fame from held cards (R-18.15) is not a gain. The additional fame does not itself trigger the ability again. | 2026-11-05 (T3.1) |
| 3 | Ketsu Onyo-0, "Encounter a patrol or contact token that is within 3 spaces of you" | Ketsu stays where she is; the patrol or contact may be up to 3 spaces away, counted along paths (R-5.29). Losing to a patrol there still lets her push it to a space adjacent to it (R-11.7). Before T3.1 the engine only offered patrols and contacts in her own space. | 2026-11-05 (T3.1) |
