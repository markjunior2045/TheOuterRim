# Interpretations awaiting the owner's review

Readings made while encoding cards or rules where the text left room for doubt. The engine follows the
reading below until the owner confirms or corrects it. When an item is settled, move the decision to
`RULES.md` (or the card data) and delete it here.

Cards are named `Name-num` (image number), as in `data/transcription/`.

| # | Where | Reading in use | Since |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | R-9.6, R-16.15, R-16.16 (credits with the current player) | Digitally, credits are **given**: one player hands an amount to another, one of them the current player, at any moment of that player's turn, from anywhere on the map, even while a decision or a combat is pending. No acceptance is asked: swapping credits for credits is a net gift, and promises are not enforced (R-9.7). | 2026-11-06 (T3.2) |
| 2 | R-16.8 to R-16.11 (the trade action) | The current player makes **one offer** per action step to one player in their space: credits and cards given, credits and cards asked. The partner accepts or refuses; either way the trade action is used (R-4.9), so a refused offer cannot be retried in the same step (negotiation happens before the offer, by talking or by giving credits). If, by the time the partner accepts, one side no longer has what was offered (credits given away meanwhile, R-16.16), nothing changes hands. | 2026-11-06 (T3.2) |
| 3 | R-14.2 and trades | A card received in a trade is **gained**: "when you gain" abilities and goals see it (GX1 Short Hauler: "gain a crew while you have 2 crew"). The receiver cannot decline it; if it does not fit, they discard until everything fits (R-14.18). | 2026-11-06 (T3.2) |
